    How safe is a song password?
    • Lyrics are public. Anyone who guesses your song can try its lines, so avoid a song you have posted about or put in a bio.
    • Tick three or more lines from different parts of the song, and aim for 16 characters or more.
    • Use different lines for each important account. For your email and bank, a password manager is still the safest home for passwords.
    • You need to remember the song, the lines and these settings. The page does not save your password or your ticked lines.

    Lyrics come from LRCLIB. Your password is built on this page and never sent anywhere. Only the song you search for goes to lrclib.net.
